The Public Site

What visitors see

Every section on the home page can be shown or hidden independently — and every word on it is editable from the admin panel, live, with no redeploy.

Modular home page

Hero, stats, trusted partners, process steps, products, portfolio, gallery, testimonials, latest articles, news, about, team, certifications, map, and contact — assembled from independent sections.

Product catalog

Filterable by category on the home page, with a dedicated page per product for sharing and direct enquiries.

Blog, News & Events

Full articles with automatic URLs. Announcements and events share one system, each clearly badged by type.

Photo gallery

The latest shots surface on the home page; the full archive is one click away on its own page.

Team directory

Members are grouped by department, with independent control over who appears on the home page versus the full roster.

Membership lookup

Visitors confirm their own status by name and postcode — no account required, and no one else's details are ever exposed.

Direct-to-WhatsApp

Every chat button opens with a message written for that exact spot on the page, not one generic greeting.

Privacy-first analytics

Visits are only ever counted after a visitor opts in via the consent banner — never before.

The Admin Command Center

What you control

One login. Every piece of content the public site shows lives in one organized place, grouped so nothing gets lost in a long menu.

Full content control

Products, Blog, Reviews, About, Team & Members, Partners, News & Events, Gallery, Stats, process steps, and contact details — all editable, all live the moment you save.

Organized navigation

Related tools are grouped into clear menus — Content, People, Insights — instead of one long unsorted list.

Enquiry inbox

Every form submission from the public site lands in one reviewable list, no email inbox required.

Visitor analytics

Daily visit counts, top countries and cities, and an interactive world map of exactly where traffic is coming from.

Account controls

Admins change their own password from inside the panel — no re-seeding, no developer required.

Safeguards by default

Deleting anything asks first — and if it would take other records with it, the confirmation says exactly how many.

Design, Yours to Choose

How it looks

Change the entire look of the site in one click — no redesign, no developer, no waiting for a new build.

Eight built-in themes

Six with independent light and dark modes; two committed dark-only looks for a bolder pitch.

Light & dark, independently

Visitors pick whichever's comfortable; their choice is remembered on return visits.

One consistent system

Every page and every component draws from the same palette — recolor once, it updates everywhere at once.

Built to Last

What you never see

The parts a visitor never notices, working exactly as carefully as the parts they do.

Self-repairing database

Every table checks and updates its own structure automatically — no separate migration step to remember.

Locked down by default

Every route is explicitly marked public or protected; anything unrecognized is refused outright, never silently exposed.

Found by search engines

Structured business data, sharable link previews, and an always-current sitemap — all generated automatically.

Never a blank crash screen

Errors and unavailable pages are branded and on-theme, not a raw stack trace or a default 404.
